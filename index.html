About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at UC San Diego, advised by Professor Bo Li. I expect to complete my Ph.D. by June 2027.

My research interests lie in the analysis of partial differential equations and the calculus of variations, with applications in biology and physics. My recent work focuses on continuum models of reaction–diffusion, electrostatic interactions, and coarsening dynamics. I am also interested in numerical methods for PDEs and variational problems.

Before coming to UC San Diego, I completed Part III of the Mathematical Tripos at the University of Cambridge and a B.Sc. (Honours) in Mathematics at the University of Delhi.

I am on the job market for postdoctoral and teaching positions in mathematics.
