Research

My recent work concerns PDE-constrained optimization in reaction–diffusion and electrostatic models. I investigate how diffusion and nonlinear interactions determine optimal spatial distributions, with particular attention to concentration, singular limits, and boundary layers. Current projects address optimal enzyme placement, regularization of reaction maximization, and electrostatic free energies with concentration-dependent dielectrics.

Publications and Preprints

  1. Maximizing Reaction via Spatial Localization: A PDE-Constrained Variational Approach

    With B. Li. Preprint, 2026. [arXiv]
    Revised and resubmitted to SIAM Journal on Mathematical Analysis.

    We construct concentrating sequences that approach the optimal reaction rate, prove nonexistence of local and global maximizers, and establish existence for a regularized model.

  2. Maximizing Reaction via Spatial Localization: Concentration, Concavity, and Absence of Critical Points

    With B. Li. Preprint, 2026.

    We characterize maximizing sequences, determine the reaction rates attainable by boundary concentration, and prove the absence of critical points, with concavity results distinguishing linear and Michaelis–Menten kinetics.
